---
title: Swap email transport
description: Keep EmailService; replace the Bento Layer with another provider adapter.
---

Goal: change how mail is delivered without rewriting auth or invitation call sites.

## Keep

- `EmailService` methods (`sendVerificationEmail`, `sendPasswordResetEmail`, `sendInvitationEmail`)
- React Email templates in `packages/email`
- Call sites that depend on the port

## Change

1. Open `apps/api/src/platform/email/email-service.ts`.
2. Study `ConsoleEmailLive` and `BentoEmailLive` as the adapter pattern.
3. Implement a new Layer that delivers `EmailMessage` through your provider (Resend, Postmark, Cloudflare Email Routing, and so on).
4. Teach `emailLiveFromEnv` (or the edge equivalent) to select your Layer when your env vars are present.
5. Leave Bento selection behind your own credentials check, or remove it if the clone will never use Bento.
6. Update [Capabilities](/docs/concepts/capabilities) notes in your clone's README if the default vendor story changes.

## Checks

- Empty credentials still take the console path (or your explicit local adapter).
- A verification email still renders the same template subject and body.
- Domain modules still import `EmailService`, not the provider SDK.

That is the whole swap. Ports stay; Layers move.
